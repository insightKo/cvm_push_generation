Метаданные до выкладки (только чтение sys.columns; тип + точность), для П-проверок выше:
SELECT OBJECT_NAME(c.object_id) AS T, c.name, t.name AS TYPE_NAME, c.max_length, c.precision, c.scale, c.is_nullable
FROM sys.columns AS c (nolock) JOIN sys.types AS t (nolock) ON t.user_type_id = c.user_type_id
WHERE (c.object_id = OBJECT_ID(N'dbo.I_CHECKHEADER') AND c.name IN (N'YEAR_MONTH', N'WEEKNUMBER', N'ID_SHOP', N'DISCOUNT',
        N'REAL_DISCOUNT', N'SKU', N'CLOCK', N'TIME', N'DAYTYPE'))
   OR (c.object_id = OBJECT_ID(N'dbo.I_CHECK') AND c.name IN (N'DATA', N'ID_CONTACT', N'ID_ORGANIZATION', N'ID_COMPANY',
        N'ID_CATEGORY_2', N'ID_CATEGORY_3', N'ID_CATEGORY_4', N'ID_CATEGORY_5'))
   OR (c.object_id IN (OBJECT_ID(N'dbo.fav_product'), OBJECT_ID(N'dbo.fav_CATEGORY')) AND c.name IN (N'DATA', N'ID_CONTACT'))
ORDER BY T, c.column_id;
Ожидаемо: YEAR_MONTH, WEEKNUMBER, ID_SHOP — целые в пределах int (они объявлены int в #hist, #wk, #sd); I_CHECK.DATA — date;
I_CHECK.ID_ORGANIZATION — NOT NULL; DISCOUNT, REAL_DISCOUNT, SKU, CLOCK, TIME — не float/real. Иное: несовпадение типа даст
ошибку преобразования → 'error', без ALTER (одна попытка потеряна); float — см. раздел 3.
